    How we found these ridges

    Every ridge on the map rises at least 100 m above its surroundings, runs for at least 2 km, has an average wind of at least 7 m/s at 50 m with most of it blowing across the crest, has a road or track within 1 km, and lies at least 3 km from any existing or planned wind farm. Protected areas, distance to homes, grid connection and airports are not screened yet: a site check covers them.

    How to use the map

    1. Choose a country.
    2. Pick a ridge on the map or from the list.
    3. Request a site check. We reply within 5 working days.

    About these numbers. Modelled potential is a gross estimate from public wind and terrain data, with rotors along the whole crest. It is a technical maximum, not a build plan: real projects use a fraction of it. Figures are before wake, availability and electrical losses, and real yield depends on micro-siting, grid capacity and permitting. Uncertainty is roughly ±25–30%. Preliminary estimates have not yet had our full modelling check. Not a commitment.
